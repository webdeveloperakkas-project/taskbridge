HELP CENTER

Frequently Asked Questions

Choose a question to read helpful information.

How do I update my account email or password?

Open Settings from your profile menu. In Account Settings, use Change Email and complete the verification steps. To update your password, open Security & Privacy, enter your current password, then choose and confirm your new password.

Why is my deposit or withdrawal taking time?

Payment processing times can vary by payment method and review requirements. Check your wallet transaction history for the latest status. If a transaction remains pending longer than expected, send us its reference and details using the form below. Never share your password or verification codes.

Why is my job waiting for approval?

New jobs may require an administrator review before they become available to workers. Check your job status in the dashboard. If the job has been pending unusually long, include its Job ID in your support message.

When will I receive payment for a completed task?

Task rewards are credited after the submission is reviewed and approved. You can check the submission status and your available wallet balance from the dashboard. If an approved reward is missing, include the Job ID and submission details.

How can I protect my account and personal information?

Use a strong, unique password and keep your email account secure. Never share your password, OTP, or recovery codes. Contact support immediately if you notice an unfamiliar login or activity on your account.
